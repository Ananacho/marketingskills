# Filming Checklist — Transformation Build

What to film at each stage of a vehicle build. Capture these clips and you'll always have content.

---

## Day 1 — Vehicle Arrives

- [ ] Exterior: front 3/4, rear 3/4, both sides
- [ ] Interior: bare/empty — wide shot from rear door
- [ ] Close-up: odometer, VIN plate, any notable condition
- [ ] "Day 1" voiceover or text overlay with project brief

**Why:** Establishes the before. Every transformation needs a before.

---

## During Build — Key Milestones

### Structural / Floor
- [ ] Floor panels being cut or laid
- [ ] Seat rail installation (close-up of welding or bolting)
- [ ] Before/after of floor section

### Upholstery
- [ ] Seat frame on workbench
- [ ] Fabric being cut
- [ ] Finished seat installed in vehicle
- [ ] Full row of seats installed (wide shot)

### Electrical / Lighting
- [ ] Wiring harness being routed
- [ ] Lights coming on for first time (interior lit up)
- [ ] Control panel or switches

### Exterior / Bodywork
- [ ] Any custom bodywork or trim
- [ ] Wrapping or painting (time-lapse if possible)

### Refrigerated / Special Configs
- [ ] Partition wall installation
- [ ] Refrigeration unit being fitted
- [ ] Temperature readout on finished unit

---

## Final Day — Completion

- [ ] Full walkaround: exterior, all sides
- [ ] Slide door opening reveal
- [ ] Interior walkaround from rear to front: floor → seats → overhead → front
- [ ] Each feature highlighted: lights, AC vents, headrests, windows
- [ ] CE plate being affixed (always film this — trust signal)
- [ ] Vehicle rolling out of workshop (great outro shot)

---

## Delivery Day

- [ ] Client arriving (ask permission to film)
- [ ] First moment client sees the vehicle
- [ ] Client stepping inside for first time
- [ ] Client testing features (seats, lights, doors)
- [ ] Handover: keys changing hands
- [ ] Handshake / smiling photo moment
- [ ] Vehicle departing the workshop

**Tip:** Tell the client the day before you'd like to film the pickup. Most are happy to — they're proud too.

---

## Phone Filming Tips

- **Stabilize:** Use both hands or a cheap gimbal. Shaky footage kills views.
- **Light:** Film near open workshop doors when possible. Interior lighting alone is usually not enough.
- **Orientation:** Vertical (9:16) for TikTok/Reels. Horizontal for YouTube. Shoot vertical by default.
- **Audio:** Workshop noise is fine for process clips. Voiceover recorded separately sounds cleaner.
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum. 4K if your phone supports it.
- **Close-ups:** Go closer than feels comfortable. Detail shots outperform wide shots in this niche.

---

## Quick Reference — Clips Per Build

| Stage | Minimum Clips | Target Clips |
|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Day 1 — Bare vehicle | 3 | 6 |
| Build process | 6 | 15 |
| Completion walkaround | 5 | 10 |
| Delivery day | 3 | 8 |
| **Total per build** | **17** | **39** |

With 17 clips per build, you can create:
- 1 full transformation video
- 2–3 process clips
- 1 spec reveal
- 1 delivery day video

With 39 clips, add a 4–5 part build series.
